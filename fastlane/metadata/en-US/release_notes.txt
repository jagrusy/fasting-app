What's new in Solstice:
• Quick-Select Fasting Plans: Tap the arrow next to Start Fast to launch Daily, Extended (24–72h), or Custom (12–72h) fasts in one tap.
• Flexible Mid-Fast Controls: Tap Goal Target to extend by +1 hour or change your goal anytime, and edit start dates across multiple days.
• Smarter Reminders & Eating Window: Daily start reminders automatically pause once a fast begins, with a live eating-window badge between fasts.
• Watch, Widget & Streak Polish: More reliable Apple Watch sync, Save/Discard prompt when ending early on Watch, refreshed Control Center & Eating Window widgets, and overnight streak fixes.
