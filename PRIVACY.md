# Privacy Policy for Solstice: Intermittent Fasting

**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

**Solstice** ("the App") is developed as a private, lightweight, on-device utility for tracking intermittent fasting routines. Your privacy is paramount.

---

## 1. 100% On-Device Architecture
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register an email address, or log in to use Solstice.
- **Local Data Storage:** All fasting history, timestamps, streaks, and user preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers:** Solstice does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Meta Pixel, or Mixpanel).

---

## 2. Information We Collect
**We collect zero personal data.**
- No personally identifiable information (PII)
- No health or biometric metrics
- No location or GPS data
- No IP addresses or device identifiers

---

## 3. System Permissions
Solstice may request the following iOS permissions strictly for local functionality:
- **Notifications (`UNUserNotificationCenter`):** Used exclusively to alert you locally on your device when your fasting goal has been reached or when your eating window begins. Notification scheduling is handled entirely on-device and never communicates with external push notification servers.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & External Links
- The App includes links in Settings to reputable educational medical resources (e.g., Johns Hopkins Medicine, Harvard Health, Mayo Clinic).
- When you tap these links, you will navigate to their respective websites via Safari, which operate under their own privacy policies.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Solstice is not intended for individuals under the age of 18 or those with specific medical contraindications. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

---

## 6. Medical Disclaimer
Solstice is an educational and habit-tracking utility and does not provide medical advice. Consult a licensed physician or healthcare provider before starting any fasting protocol.
