Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Solstice ("we", "our", or "the App") is built on a fundamental principle: your health and habit data belongs exclusively to you. We believe fasting is a personal wellness journey that requires absolute privacy.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • 100% On-Device: All your fasting timers, historical logs, mood ratings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple Core Data.
  • No Cloud Accounts: Solstice does not require you to create an account, sign in with an email, or connect any social profile.
  • No Server Collection: We do not operate remote servers that collect, store, or transmit your fasting data.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

Solstice contains zero third-party tracking frameworks, advertising SDKs, or behavioral telemetry. We do not track your location, advertising identifier (IDFA), or device fingerprints.

4. App Store Connect Analytics

Apple may provide developers with aggregate, anonymized diagnostics (such as crash logs or opt-in app performance metrics) if you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings. This data is fully anonymized by Apple.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Solstice is designed for general habit tracking and educational wellness purposes only. It is not intended as medical advice or a diagnostic tool. Consult a qualified physician before starting any fasting regimen.
