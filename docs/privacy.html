Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

Solstice ("we", "our", or "the App") was founded on an uncompromising principle: your fasting, metabolic, and nutrition records belong strictly to you. Unlike incumbent health apps that monetize by reselling user habits or tracking data to advertisers, Solstice operates without tracking frameworks or remote data harvesting.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

  • 100% On-Device: All your fasting timers, biological stage records, history heatmaps, notes, and user settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.
  • Zero Accounts or Social Logins: You never need to create an account, register an email address, or link Apple ID to use Solstice's core fasting tools.
  • Zero Central Servers: We do not operate databases that log when you fast, what protocols you choose, or your health milestones.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry

Solstice contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, data broker trackers, or behavioral telemetry frameworks (such as Facebook SDK, Google Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel). We never request or track your Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

4. Solstice AI Features & Future Services

For optional future premium services (such as Solstice AI meal photo scanning and nutritional journaling):

  • Images and food queries are processed using on-device Apple Intelligence where supported, or ephemeral, privacy-sanitized endpoints without retaining personal identifiers.
  • Your meal logs and photos will never be sold, leased, or utilized to train commercial advertising models.

5. App Store Diagnostic Analytics

If you have voluntarily enabled "Share With App Developers" in your iOS device settings, Apple provides developers with anonymized, aggregate diagnostic information (e.g. crash logs and OS version distribution). This telemetry is completely depersonalized by Apple.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Solstice is designed for general wellness habit tracking and educational purposes. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment advice, or clinical evaluations. Always consult a licensed medical physician before initiating prolonged fasting protocols.
