Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start, pause, or end a fast?

From the iOS app, tap the central circular button to start. When you're ready to break your fast, tap "End Fast" and optionally rate your mood or notes. You can also start/end fasts with one tap from the Apple Watch app or the iOS 18 Control Center toggle.

How do I set up StandBy Nightstand Mode?

Connect your iPhone to a MagSafe or Qi charger and place it horizontally (landscape). Swipe through your StandBy widgets until you reach the widget editor, tap the '+' icon, and select Solstice. You'll now have an ambient, glowing metabolic timer while you sleep.

How do I add Solstice to my Apple Watch face?

Touch and hold your Apple Watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications screen, tap a complication slot, and scroll to select Solstice. Solstice supports circular, corner, rectangular, and inline complications.

How do I switch protocols (e.g. 16:8 to 20:4 or OMAD)?

Open Settings > Protocol & Schedule. Choose any standard preset (14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, 23:1 OMAD) or drag the 24-hour circular dial wheel to set any custom duration up to 72 hours.

Where is my health data stored?

All data is stored exclusively on your device in Apple Core Data. Solstice does not use remote cloud servers, accounts, or third-party analytics trackers.

When is Solstice AI Meal Tracking launching?

We are actively developing Solstice AI for "The Feeding Window" (photo-to-macros meal scanning, break-fast digestion scoring, and smart food journaling). Join the VIP Beta Waitlist on the homepage to be the first to test it.
