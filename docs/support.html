Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start or end a fast?

On the Fast tab, tap the large circular button labeled "Start Fast". When your fasting window is complete, tap "End Fast" and log your optional mood rating.

How do I switch fasting protocols (e.g. 16:8 to Warrior 20:4)?

Go to Settings > Fasting Plan. You can choose from popular presets or customize your target window using the 24-hour interactive dial.

Are my fasting logs backed up to iCloud?

All data is stored locally in on-device Core Data and backed up automatically whenever your iPhone performs an encrypted local or iCloud Device Backup.

Is Solstice really free?

Yes! Solstice is a 100% free, subscription-free fasting utility with no advertisements.
