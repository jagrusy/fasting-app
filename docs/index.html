The Science of Your Fast

Solstice calculates your body's biological transitions in real time.

0 – 4 Hours

🩸 Blood Sugar Reset

Insulin levels fall, digestion completes, and blood glucose stabilizes.

4 – 12 Hours

⚡️ Glycogen Depletion

Liver stores are consumed as energy sources shift toward fat stores.

12 – 18 Hours

🔥 Fat Burning & Ketosis

Fatty acids are broken down into ketones for clean mental and physical fuel.

18 – 24 Hours

🧬 Autophagy & Renewal

Cellular cleanup initiates, recycling damaged proteins and organelles.

24+ Hours

🧠 Deep Ketosis & HGH

Growth hormone surges and systemic inflammation markers drop.
